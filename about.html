Our Story

Precision Piano has served midcoast Maine for over 25 years, tuning, repairing and restoring pianos for churches, music centers, halls and private clients from the coast inland to Bangor.

Precision Piano was founded in 1985 by Frank Fischer, a graduate of the North Bennet Street School. Today the team includes technicians trained in that same tradition of hands-on craftsmanship, dedicated to keeping every piano we service sounding its best.
