Piano Services

Professional piano care for homes, studios, churches, schools, performance spaces and institutions throughout Midcoast and Downeast Maine.

Thoughtful Care for Every Piano

Every piano has its own history, condition, character and needs. Our approach is to understand the instrument first, then provide the level of care that will best serve the piano and its owner.

From a precision tuning to complete restoration, we bring careful attention to the details that make a piano play, respond and sound its best.

  • Piano Tuning

    Every tuning deserves concert-level service. We approach each instrument with careful attention to pitch, stability, tone and the condition of the piano itself.

    We tune upright and grand pianos in homes, studios, churches, schools, halls and other musical spaces.

  • Repairs, Regulation & Voicing

    For clients who appreciate the highest level of service, we provide detailed attention to both the mechanical and musical aspects of the instrument.

    Repairs address mechanical problems and worn components. Regulation brings the action into precise adjustment for consistency, responsiveness and control. Voicing refines the character and balance of the piano's tone.

  • Complete Restoration

    A complete restoration is a significant undertaking that can give a worthy instrument a new life.

    Depending on the piano, restoration may include rebuilding the action, soundboard and bridges, restringing, refinishing and restoring the instrument's mechanical and musical systems.

    We evaluate each instrument individually and discuss what level of restoration makes sense for the piano and your goals.

  • Piano Evaluation

    Considering purchasing a piano or wondering what your existing instrument needs? An evaluation can help you understand its condition, potential problems and future maintenance needs.

    We can help you make informed decisions about a piano before you commit to purchasing it or undertaking significant work.

  • Piano Moving

    Pianos are heavy, delicate instruments that require careful handling. Professional moving helps protect the cabinet, action, pedals, legs and other vulnerable components.

    We provide careful piano moving and handling for moves within the home as well as transportation between locations.
